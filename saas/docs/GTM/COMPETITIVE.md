# Competitive map (short)

**Our category:** Entra ↔ MDM reconciliation with an Issues inbox — not CMDB discovery, not hardware logistics.

## Layers (do not conflate)

| Layer | Job | Examples | Overlap with us |
|-------|-----|----------|-----------------|
| Discovery / CMDB | Find & model infra | **Virima**, Lansweeper, Flexera, ServiceNow CMDB | Low — different job |
| ITAM registry | Who has what, warranty, barcode | Snipe-IT, BlueTally, AssetSonar, InvGate | Medium — they sync MDM; Issues is secondary |
| **Reconciliation** | Drift, owner mismatch, exception inbox | **Us**, Korvini, Vertask | **Direct** |
| DaaS / logistics | Buy, ship, retrieve devices | **Workwize**, Firstbase, GroWrk, allwhere, Deel IT | Adjacent — partner, not rival |

## Named vendors you will hear

- **Virima** — CMDB + discovery-led ITAM; Intune/Entra/Jamf as CI feed (~$395/mo). Pitch: they map the estate; we tell you what is wrong between identity and device ownership today.
- **Workwize** (+ Firstbase / GroWrk / allwhere / Deel IT) — global device logistics + MDM status. Pitch: they move hardware; we reconcile truth. Often complementary.
- **Korvini** — closest product rival (Jamf+Intune+Entra reconciliation). Differentiate on MDM breadth, CSV-first pilots, Entra rule clarity, EU pricing/GDPR packaging.
- **Vertask / BlueTally / Harmony** — ITAM or suite with gap detection; we stay Issues-first and cheaper to start (€79 Starter vs BlueTally $199+).

## One-liner for objections

> “Virima is CMDB. Workwize is logistics. We are the Issues layer on top of Entra + your MDMs.”
